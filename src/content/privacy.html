You can reach us at hello@lumecrm.in.

For the data in its own LUME, the Business decides what is collected and why, and it is responsible for it (the data controller). We provide the software and act on the Business's instructions. For this website, the connection service and enquiries sent to us, we are responsible.

How LUME runs

LUME is not a shared cloud service. Each Business runs its own copy of LUME on a server it controls, at its own web address. The Business decides who gets an account and what LUME is used for, and it is responsible for the personal data in its copy of LUME, including yours. We do not receive a Business's leads, its notes or its team's activity as part of providing LUME.

The only things that leave a Business's server are those needed for the integrations that Business switches on (described below), and a licence check that sends technical details about the installation (such as its licence and software version), never lead data.

Data in LUME

About the people who use LUME

Name, email address, role, password (stored only as a one-way hash), two-step sign-in secret (encrypted), preferences, the sessions you are signed in with (device, approximate time and network address), a record of important actions in an audit log, and when you agreed to LUME's terms.

About leads

The details the Business collects about its leads: names, contact details, notes, the stage of each conversation, follow-ups, messages logged and any custom fields the Business adds. Contact details are encrypted at rest and can be masked for roles that don't need them; revealing one is recorded in the audit log.

Who sees it

Only people the Business gives accounts to, within their roles. Email from LUME (invitations, password resets, digests) goes through the email service the Business chooses. When an admin changes the business currency, LUME asks an exchange-rate service for a rate; no personal data is sent.

Google user data

A Business can let its people connect a Google account to LUME with “Continue with Google”. This is always optional, always started by you, and you see Google's own consent screen listing exactly what LUME asks for. LUME does not use Google to sign you in to LUME.

What LUME asks for, and why

Google Sheets you pick
drive.file		 Lets LUME open only the files you choose in Google's own file picker. LUME reads the rows of the spreadsheet you picked and brings them into your Business's LUME as leads, then checks it from time to time for new rows. LUME cannot see, list or open any other file in your Google Drive, and it never edits, moves or deletes your files.
Events on calendars you own, read-only
calendar.events.owned.readonly		 Lets LUME read events on the calendars you own and choose, so it can show your meetings with leads alongside those leads (for example a call booked with someone in your pipeline). LUME cannot read calendars that other people share with you, and it never creates, changes or deletes an event.
List of calendars, read-only
calendar.calendarlist.readonly		 Lets LUME show you which of your calendars you own, by name, so you can choose the ones LUME reads.

What LUME keeps

  • Your connection: the access Google grants (a refresh token), encrypted, on your Business's own server.
  • From a sheet you picked: the sheet's id and name, and the rows LUME reads from it, encrypted, plus the leads created from those rows.
  • From your calendar: only events that match a lead in your Business's LUME (by an attendee's email address or by the meeting rules your Business sets): the event's title, time, location or meeting link, and which lead it matched. All other events, including personal events, are read only to check for a match and are never stored.

How it is used

Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features described above for you and your Business, inside your Business's LUME. It is shown only to people in your Business whose role lets them see the lead it belongs to.

Who it is shared with

Nobody else. Google user data is never sold, never transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers, and never used to decide anyone's creditworthiness or for lending. It is not transferred to any third party except as needed to provide these features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of LUME with notice to you.

Disconnecting and deletion

  • Calendar: disconnecting in LUME deletes your connection and every meeting that connection brought in, straight away.
  • A sheet: removing it in LUME deletes LUME's Google access to it straight away, and LUME stops reading it. The leads and rows already brought in stay in your Business's LUME as the Business's records, like any other lead, until the Business deletes them.
  • In your Google account: you can remove LUME's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. LUME then stops reading straight away and shows the connection as needing to be connected again.
  • You can also ask your Business's admin, or us, to delete Google data held about you.

Limited Use

LUME's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • LUME uses Google user data only to provide or improve the user-facing features described in this policy.
  • LUME does not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or personalised or interest-based ads.
  • LUME does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads Google user data unless you have given permission for specific data, it is needed for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), it is needed to comply with the law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations. People in your own Business see it inside LUME only as described above.

Meta and WhatsApp

A Business can connect a WhatsApp Business number to its LUME through the WhatsApp Business Platform of Meta Platforms, Inc. (“Meta”). LUME is a Tech Provider on that platform. Connecting is always optional and off until an admin of the Business switches it on and connects the number on Meta's own sign-up screens (Facebook Login for Business), where Meta shows what LUME asks for and the person connecting decides.

What LUME asks Meta for, and why

Manage the WhatsApp account
whatsapp_business_management		 To connect the Business's number to its LUME, read the number's display number, verified business name, quality rating and messaging limit, and submit and read the Business's message templates (and whether WhatsApp approved them).
Send and receive messages
whatsapp_business_messaging		 To receive the messages people send to the connected number, and to send the messages the Business's team writes in LUME (and, only if the Business switches it on, LUME AI's messages) from that number.
Basic profile
public_profile		 Meta includes this with every Facebook Login. LUME uses the sign-in only to complete the connection. It does not read, ask for or keep the name, picture or any other detail from the Facebook profile of the person who connects.

What LUME receives

  • Messages: messages people send to the connected number, and messages the Business sends from its phone, with their time and delivery status (sent, delivered, read).
  • Photos, voice notes, videos and documents in those messages, up to 25 MB each.
  • Who wrote: the sender's WhatsApp number and the profile name they set in WhatsApp.
  • Past chats, only if the person connecting the number chooses to share them on Meta's screen. Meta may also send the contacts saved in the WhatsApp Business app; LUME doesn't keep them.
  • The number's details: its display number, its verified business name, its quality rating and messaging limit, the Business's message templates and their status, and an access token Meta grants for that number.

How it is used

  • Only to provide LUME's WhatsApp features to the Business that connected the number: putting messages on the matching lead so its team can see who replied and follow up, sending the replies and approved templates its team chooses, and showing the number's health.
  • Messages from numbers that aren't a lead wait in “Not a lead yet” and are deleted after 14 days unless someone in the Business makes them a lead.
  • If the Business switches on LUME AI, LUME can suggest next steps and summaries from a lead's history, which can include its WhatsApp messages. If the Business also starts an Autopilot run, LUME AI writes and sends WhatsApp messages to the leads the Business chose, on the Business's behalf, within the limits the Business sets. Every such message is marked as LUME AI's in LUME, a person can take over at any time, and anyone who says “stop” is marked do not contact and messaged no more.
  • Data from Meta is never sold, never transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers, never used for advertising or marketing to anyone, never used to build profiles of people for any other purpose, and never used to train AI models. It is shown only to people in the Business whose role lets them see the lead it belongs to.

Who it is shared with

  • Meta, to deliver the messages the Business sends.
  • The AI provider the Business's LUME is set up with (such as Anthropic or Google), only if the Business switches LUME AI on, only the parts of a lead's history needed for the task, never a phone number or email address, and only on the provider's paid service, whose terms forbid training on it.
  • Nobody else, except to comply with the law or with Meta's own requests about Platform Data, or as part of a merger or sale of LUME with notice to the Businesses using it.

Meta's rules

We use data received from Meta only as allowed by Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies, and LUME's WhatsApp features follow the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy. Each Business is responsible for having people's permission to message them on WhatsApp, as WhatsApp requires. Messages on WhatsApp are also covered by Meta's own terms and privacy policy, and Meta bills the Business directly for the messages it charges for.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • On the Business's own server: messages in its database and files in its own storage. The access token is encrypted there. We don't receive copies.
  • Messages already on leads stay in the Business's LUME as its records, like any other lead activity, until the Business deletes them. The Business can also choose how long LUME keeps WhatsApp files.
  • When an admin disconnects the number in LUME, LUME tells Meta to stop sending it messages, deletes the access token straight away and stops sending from that number. Removing LUME in Meta's business settings also ends LUME's access at once.

The connection service (connect.lumecrm.in)

Because every Business runs LUME at its own address, Google and Meta connect to one small service we run at connect.lumecrm.in. It holds LUME's Google and Meta app credentials, shows Google's file picker and Meta's WhatsApp sign-up, and passes your new connection back to your Business's server, sealed so only that server can open it.

  • It never receives lead data, sheet contents or calendar events.
  • WhatsApp messages for a connected number pass through it on their way from Meta to the Business's server. It checks that each one really came from Meta and forwards it straight away. It does not store messages, files or WhatsApp access tokens, and it keeps no list of numbers.
  • During a connection it holds the access Google grants in memory for at most ten minutes, then forgets it. It does not store refresh tokens.
  • When your Business's server needs a fresh access token, it asks this service, which passes the request to Google and returns the token without keeping it.
  • Like any web server, it may log technical details of requests (time, network address, the page asked for) to keep it secure and working. Those logs never contain tokens and are deleted within 30 days.

This website

lumecrm.in uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. It remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser only. Our hosting provider records technical request logs (such as network address and time) to deliver and protect the site. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

If you ask for a demo with the form on lumecrm.in, the details you type (your name, business, WhatsApp number with its country code, email if you give it, team size and how leads reach you) are kept in our own records of enquiries, only so we can reply and arrange the demo. They are never sold or shared, and we delete them on request.

Security

LUME uses encrypted connections everywhere, encrypts contact details, Google and WhatsApp connections and other secrets at rest, offers two-step sign-in, limits each role to what it needs, signs disabled accounts out everywhere and records important actions in an audit log that can't be edited. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell the affected Business without undue delay so it can tell you.

Keeping data

Data in a Business's LUME is kept for as long as the Business keeps it, and Google and WhatsApp data are deleted as described above. When a Business stops using LUME, it can export its data, and LUME is then removed from its server with everything in it. Enquiries sent to us are kept for as long as we are talking with the business that sent them, and deleted on request.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or move personal data about you, or to object to how it is used. LUME is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children.

How to have your data deleted

  1. If you messaged a business that uses LUME (on WhatsApp or otherwise), ask that business: its admin can delete your lead, your messages and your files in LUME. If you can't reach it, email hello@lumecrm.in with the business's name and the WhatsApp number or email you used. We will pass your request to the business and help it act on it.
  2. If your business connected a WhatsApp number, an admin can disconnect it in LUME (Settings, Integrations, WhatsApp, Disconnect) or remove LUME in Meta's business settings. LUME's access ends straight away and its access token is deleted. To delete the messages already in LUME, the admin deletes them there, or removes LUME from the server altogether.
  3. If you signed in with Facebook to connect a number, LUME kept nothing from your Facebook profile. You can still remove LUME at any time in Facebook's settings, under Apps and websites.
  4. If you connected a Google account, disconnect it in LUME or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, as described above.
  5. For an enquiry you sent us, or for anything else, email hello@lumecrm.in.

We reply to every deletion request within 30 days and confirm once it is done. We may ask you to show that the data is about you before deleting it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how Google user data is used, LUME will ask for your consent again before using it in the new way.
